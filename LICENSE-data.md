## Provenance and attribution of the underlying sources

The data tables are derived from public disclosures. Individual figures trace to
company filings (NI 43-101 technical reports, ASX/SEC filings, operator reports),
government and multilateral statistics (USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries, ICSG,
Cochilco, IMF WEO, World Bank), political-economy datasets (V-Dem, V-Party,
Polity5, ICRG), and the OECD inventory of export restrictions on industrial raw
materials. These upstream sources retain their own terms; CC BY 4.0 applies to
the curated tables in this repository, not to the primary sources they summarize.

Where a source citation is still being finalized before journal submission, the
corresponding cell is marked `[CHECK]`; see the README's "Data status" note.
